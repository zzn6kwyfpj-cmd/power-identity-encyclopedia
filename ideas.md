# Power, Identity, and Contested Origins — Website Design

## Chosen Approach: The Royal Archive

**Design Movement:** Neo-Victorian Digital Archive meets Museum Catalog
**Core Principles:**
1. Deep midnight navy (#0a1118) as the primary background — commanding, authoritative, timeless
2. Antique gold (#d4af37) as the accent — royalty, legacy, illuminated manuscripts
3. Serif typography (Cinzel for headings, Cormorant Garamond for body) — scholarly, elegant, literary
4. Generous white space and ornamental dividers — museum-quality breathing room

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep midnight navy (#0a1118) — the color of night sky and ancient ink
- Primary accent: Antique gold (#d4af37) — the color of illuminated manuscripts and royal seals
- Secondary accent: Deep crimson (#8b1a1a) — the color of blood, sacrifice, and resistance
- Text: Soft silver-cream (#e2e8f0) — readable against dark backgrounds, warm and inviting
- Muted text: Warm gray (#94a3b8)

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width cinematic hero sections with parallax-style depth
- Asymmetric chapter cards with gold left-border accents
- Sidebar navigation for chapter browsing
- Magazine-style chapter pages with floating portrait images

**Signature Elements:**
1. Gold ornamental dividers (✦ ✦ ✦) between sections
2. Drop caps at the start of each chapter
3. Pull quote boxes with crimson left borders
4. Evidence tier badges (green/gold/crimson)

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cinzel (Google Fonts) — regal, commanding
- Body: Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts) — literary, flowing
- Mono: Source Code Pro — for citations and references

**Brand Essence:** The definitive educational resource on American power, identity, and contested origins — for scholars, students, and anyone seeking the truth.

**Brand Voice:** Authoritative but accessible. Scholarly but human. The voice of a historian who has read everything and is finally telling the whole truth.

## Style Decisions
- Dark theme throughout — no light mode
- Gold borders on chapter cards, not rounded corners
- All images use object-fit: contain with gold frames
- Navigation: sticky top bar with chapter dropdown
- Mobile: collapsible sidebar navigation
